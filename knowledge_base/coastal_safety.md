# Coastal and severe-weather safety starter guide

This starter guide provides general preparedness information. It is not a forecast,
official warning, evacuation order, or substitute for instructions from the
Kerala State Disaster Management Authority (KSDMA), district administration,
India Meteorological Department (IMD), police, or emergency responders.
Follow current local instructions even if they differ from this general guide.
Call India's emergency number 112 if there is immediate danger.

## Cyclone and severe storm

Monitor current IMD forecasts and official KSDMA or district-administration
notices. If authorities order evacuation, leave promptly using the route and
designated shelter they specify. Before conditions worsen, bring loose outdoor
items inside, charge phones, prepare essential medicines and documents, and
stay away from the shoreline. During strong winds, stay inside a sturdy
building, away from windows and glass. Do not go outdoors during a temporary
lull; wait for an official all-clear before leaving or returning to an evacuated
area.

## Flooding and heavy rain

Move early to higher ground when directed or when water is rising around you.
Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater: depth and current can be hard to
judge, and roads may be damaged or electrically hazardous. Keep away from fallen
power lines and report them to the utility or emergency services. Do not touch
electrical equipment while standing in water. Follow local instructions about
safe drinking water, cleanup, and when it is safe to return.

## Tsunami and sudden coastal inundation

After a strong or long earthquake near the coast, or an official tsunami or
coastal-inundation warning, move immediately inland or to higher ground using
official evacuation routes. Do not go to the beach to watch the sea and do not
wait for visible waves before moving. Stay away from the coast until authorities
issue an all-clear; dangerous surges may occur more than once.

## Lightning and thunderstorms

At the first thunder, move into a substantial enclosed building or a fully
enclosed hard-topped vehicle, then remain there until at least 30 minutes after
the last thunder. Avoid open areas, hilltops, beaches, water, isolated trees,
and metal fences during a storm. If no safe shelter is reachable, get away from
isolated tall objects and water, crouch low with feet together, and minimize
contact with the ground; this is a last resort, not a guarantee of safety.

## Landslide warning signs

Watch for new ground cracks, leaning trees or poles, unusual changes in nearby
water flow, or rumbling and falling debris on a slope. If you see signs of
movement or receive an official warning, leave the threatened slope area
promptly if you can do so safely, warn others, and contact local authorities.
Do not enter a slide area to retrieve belongings; follow responder instructions
about routes and return.

## Household emergency preparation

Keep a reachable go-bag with drinking water, ready-to-eat food, regular
medicines, first-aid supplies, a torch and spare batteries, a charged power
bank, essential clothing, hygiene supplies, copies of important documents in a
waterproof covering, and emergency contact information. Agree on a family
meeting point and an out-of-area contact. Check official alerts, plan how
children, older adults, people with disabilities, and pets will evacuate, and
keep essential items ready to leave. Do not delay an evacuation order to pack.

## Official information and emergency help

For live weather forecasts and cyclone information, use the India Meteorological
Department (IMD) website:
https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/cycloneinformation.php

For Kerala state disaster-management information, use the Kerala State Disaster
Management Authority (KSDMA):
https://sdma.kerala.gov.in/

For immediate danger in India, call 112. The chatbot's document guidance does
not confirm that a warning is active, identify a currently open shelter, or
replace a local authority's evacuation instructions.
